# Day 6 — Twelve Customer Discovery Questions

**Variant:** 3 — Parcel Logistics

**Client brief:** "Customers phone us about delays and damage. Can an assistant help them first?"

## Discovery Questions

1. How many customer calls did your team receive last week?

2. What were the most common reasons customers contacted your team during the past month?

3. Can you walk me through the last delayed-parcel complaint your team handled?

4. What happened the last time a customer reported a damaged parcel?

5. How long did your team spend resolving the most recent delivery-delay complaint?

6. What information did your support agent check when responding to the last parcel-tracking enquiry?

7. What happened the last time a customer needed assistance outside working hours?

8. How did customers check their parcel status before contacting your team in recent cases?

9. Can you describe a recent complaint that required supervisor involvement?

10. What happened during the last complaint that required multiple customer follow-ups?

11. What methods have you previously tried to reduce repetitive customer enquiries, and what were the results?

12. How did your team determine whether the last resolved customer complaint was handled successfully?

## Mom Test Approach

These questions focus on actual customer interactions, existing processes, past problems, and measurable evidence.

They avoid asking hypothetical questions such as "Would you use an AI chatbot?" or "Do you think AI would improve customer support?"

The objective is to understand the client's real operational problems before recommending a solution.
